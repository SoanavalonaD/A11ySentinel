Action required · WCAG 1.1.1 · image-alt

This patch needs a person's words

Generated patch — placeholder present

<img src="/img/team-photo-final-v2.jpg" class="team-hero" alt="TODO: describe this image">

Why the model stopped

The image is a photograph of people. Nothing in the surrounding source says who they are or why the photo is on this page, so any description the model wrote would be invented. Inventing it would be worse than the original violation: a screen reader user would be told something confidently false.

What to supply

  • Describe what the photograph shows and why it appears here — e.g. “The six-person A11ySentinel team outside the Antananarivo office”.
  • Do not start with “image of” — the screen reader already says that.
  • If the image is decorative and the surrounding text already carries the meaning, use alt="".
